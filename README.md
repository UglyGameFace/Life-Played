Life Played

Your life builds the world.

Life Played is a cross-platform 3D life RPG that turns real-world progress into meaningful game progression.

Goals, habits, projects, focus sessions, errands, achievements, shopping needs, real-world adventures, and major life milestones can shape a persistent personalized world containing characters, companions, buildings, stories, factions, collectibles, and long-term progression.

Life Played is designed around one central idea:

«What you do in real life should matter inside the game.»

Product Vision

Life Played combines:

- A serious life-planning and productivity system
- A persistent stylized 3D RPG world
- Personalized characters, companions, stories, and visual identities
- AI-assisted goal and quest planning
- Real-world exploration and optional location-based adventures
- Personalized deal, coupon, cashback, and shopping intelligence
- Merchant-funded Sponsored Quests and rewards
- A permanent Chronicle of real-world accomplishments
- Long-running stories, seasons, factions, live events, and evolving worlds

The player's real-life actions drive progression rather than existing beside the game as an unrelated checklist.

Core Experience

Real-life activity flows through the Life Played systems:

Real Life → Goals & Actions → Quest Engine → Progression → Character & World Growth → New Stories & Adventures

Major real-world goals can become multi-stage campaigns. Completing them can change the player's world permanently through new buildings, landmarks, companion developments, story events, trophies, and Chronicle entries.

Personalized Worlds

Life Played is not locked to one aesthetic.

Players can choose experiences such as:

- Cozy / Nature
- Fantasy / Adventure
- Coder / Hacker / Tech
- Science Fiction
- Dark / Arcane
- Modern / Minimal

Each identity is intended to have its own complete world, story, characters, factions, companions, locations, visual language, and long-running narrative.

Individual stories can end.

The world does not.

New sagas, regions, personal campaigns, seasonal stories, world events, factions, companions, and expansions allow progression to continue without invalidating previous accomplishments.

Real-World Commerce

Life Played can optionally connect real needs to useful commercial opportunities.

A player's explicit goals, shopping lists, preferences, and opted-in activity can help identify relevant:

- Deals
- Coupons
- Cashback
- Price drops
- Affiliate offers
- Sponsored Quests
- Nearby merchant rewards

The system is designed to prioritize genuine player need and savings before developer revenue.

Large purchases may create more meaningful game rewards and real-world savings, but spending money must never become a direct substitute for genuine progression.

Privacy & Trust

Location, commerce, and behavioral data must be treated as evidence used to provide player-facing features, not as an excuse to build an invasive surveillance product.

Core gameplay must remain usable without continuous location access.

Real-world rewards use layered validation including location evidence, purchase verification, device/app integrity, duplicate protection, fraud analysis, and fallback verification paths for legitimate users when mobile operating systems fail to track an event correctly.

Platform Direction

Life Played is intended for:

- Google Play
- Apple App Store

The current client direction is Unity, allowing the product to support real-time 3D environments, characters, companions, animation, weather, dynamic lighting, visual effects, cinematic progression moments, and scalable mobile graphics.

Backend systems will remain modular and server-authoritative where progression, commerce, purchases, rewards, and other valuable state are concerned.

Development Principle

Life Played is not a task manager with RPG decorations.

It is a living game world powered by what the player actually does.
